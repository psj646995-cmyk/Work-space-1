# Hangeul Classroom (한글 교실)

An English-language version of 한글 탐험대 for adult learners, for use in Korean classes such as 세종학당.
Open `index.html` in Chrome, Safari or Edge. It works with no internet (fonts are embedded).

- **Letters**: tap magnet letters to build a syllable. Each letter shows its sound in English letters
  (ㄱ g/k, ㅏ a), its name (giyeok), and an English hint (ㅏ = "ah" as in father). The syllable is read
  aloud by the device's Korean voice and shown with its romanization (각 [gak]).
- **Mouth shape**: an animated mouth for the initial consonant, vowel and final consonant, with English tips.
- **Spelling practice**: four levels of 10 words each; spell the word for the picture one syllable at a time.
- **Quiz**: 10 questions per round, three types (picture → word, listen → picture, word → meaning),
  with a review of missed words. Number keys 1–4 answer and Enter goes to the next question.
- **Show romanization** can be switched off for students who no longer need it.

Pictures are chalk-style line drawings made for this page (no emoji).
